Fair questions.

Setup, providers, and limits are in the README.

What does it cost?

Mossad is free. If you use cloud AI providers, they bill you directly: cloud transcription is about $0.22 to $0.36 an hour of audio, and the notes model adds its own cost. Setup shows the cost per hour of your choices. With models on your Mac, it costs nothing to run.

Does a bot join my meetings?

No. Nothing joins the call and nothing shows to the other people. That is also why telling them is your job.

Do I have to tell people that I record?

Yes. Many places require the consent of one or of all people on a call, and voice fingerprints can be biometric data under some laws. Read Lawful use in the README, and ask a lawyer if you are not sure.

Which call apps does it work with?

Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet, in their apps or in Chrome, Arc, Safari, Edge, and Brave. In a browser that it cannot read, such as Firefox, it tells you once and offers to record. You can also record a meeting in a room by hand.

Can it run with no cloud at all?

Yes. Choose transcription and a notes model that run on your Mac, and your audio and text stay on it. Quality and speed then depend on the model and your Mac.

Is there an iPhone app?

Yes: your meetings, chat, people, and the coach, and a recorder for meetings in a room. It is not in the App Store. You build it from the source with Xcode.

What if it gets something wrong?

Rename a voice and every note updates. Edit a title, regenerate a summary, or transcribe a meeting again. Summaries, coaching, and person notes come from software and can be wrong, so check them before you act on them.
