You stay in control

  • Choose models that run on your Mac, and your audio and text stay on it.
  • Turn off voice recognition, or delete all saved voices, in Settings.
  • Pause or stop any call, skip a call to delete it, and mark a meeting or a whole series as do not record.
  • Read every line: Mossad is open source under the MIT License, in its repository.

The folders and files are listed under Where your data lives in the README.

This website

This site sets no cookies and loads nothing from another company. Its fonts are served from this site.

You are the one recording

Mossad does not announce itself on a call. Tell the people you meet, and get consent where the law requires it. Voice fingerprints can be biometric data under some laws. Read Lawful use
